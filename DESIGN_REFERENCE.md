# Design benchmark — V7

The redesign uses the two requested official destination portals as benchmarks for **interaction model, hierarchy and editorial quality**, without copying their proprietary layouts or brand assets.

## Tourism New Zealand — patterns adopted

- Planning-oriented information architecture: places, things to do and trip planning are surfaced early.
- Strong top-level navigation and high-priority planning CTA.
- Full-width campaign storytelling with a concise hero proposition.
- Experience discovery presented as visually distinct modules.
- Practical planning content sits alongside inspiration rather than being hidden at the bottom.

## Visit Iceland — patterns adopted

- Large photography/video with restrained interface chrome.
- Editorial article/destination rhythm rather than a generic marketing landing page.
- Region/destination exploration as a core navigation concept.
- Culture, trip suggestions and planning are treated as first-class content.
- Minimal typography and generous scale make imagery the primary brand carrier.

## Chubut interpretation

The site uses a near-black system (`#050505`, `#0d0d0d`) with off-white text and a muted stone accent. This keeps the requested high-value black identity while allowing Chubut landscapes to provide the color. Typography pairs a neutral sans for navigation/body with a high-contrast editorial serif for destination headlines. The hero is intentionally reduced to a single cinematic YouTube background to avoid carousel noise.
